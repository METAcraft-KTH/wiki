A **Pirkko** is a small, weak and slow-moving hostile mob mainly found in caves. When killed, it drops itself as an item that can be placed as a decorative block.

## Colors

Pirkkos come in 19 colors: the 16 vanilla colors plus the three chapter colors from the new [[dyes]] (Cerise, Laser Violet and RGB).

Pirkko items cannot be crafted or dyed, so the only way to collect a color is to find and kill a Pirkko of that color.

## Advancements

There are advancements for killing Pirkkos.
