Functional changes made to the METAcraft SMP compared to vanilla Minecraft. Want to add or remove features? Post on Discord in #general and we can bring it up in #polls!

## Anvils

Anvils don't increase the XP cost when repairing or renaming an item, and the maximum XP cost never goes above 39 levels. This means *Mending* isn't as necessary as in vanilla, since there's no upper limit on how many times you can repair an item.

## Villager trading

Villager trading uses [Villager Trade Rebalance](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager_Trade_Rebalance), so you have to go to different biomes to get different trades.

## Silenced mobs

Mobs can be silenced by naming them "Silence me" with a name tag. This can be useful for, for example, secret bases.

## Nether portals

Nether portals can be activated in any size and shape you want, not just rectangular.

## Sand

Sand can be renewed by killing Husks (desert zombies), which now also spawn in Desert Temples.

## Endermen

Endermen cannot pick up blocks.

## Pets

Pets cannot be hurt by their owner or other players the pet trusts. As the owner, you can choose which other players the pet should trust by sneaking and right-clicking the pet. If a pet trusts you, you can decide whether it should sit or stand, and the pet will never attack you.

## Parrots

Parrots can be bred and healed with seeds.

## Leads

Tamed pets that are standing and leashed animals (or boats and everything they contain) will follow the player through portals.

## Minecarts

Minecarts have had their speed increased from 8 blocks/second to 40 blocks/second. Using ice, packed ice or blue ice increases the speed by 5, 10 and 20 blocks/second respectively. If you need the old mechanics for, say, a machine, you can convert minecarts into "Slowcarts" by placing them alone in a crafting grid.
