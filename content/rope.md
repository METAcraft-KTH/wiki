**Rope** holds on to the side or underside of any solid block. It hangs straight down as far as you like, and reaches up to seven blocks across a gap. Lanterns and hanging signs hang from it.

## Crafting

{{< image src="booklet/recipe/rope.png" caption="Rope crafting recipe" width="250" align="none" >}}

Makes four. A wheat and four string, anywhere in the grid.

## Placing it

Right-click a block to start a rope. Right-click the rope again and the new piece goes on the bottom end of the line, however long it is: the quick way to let a rope down a shaft. **Sneak** to put it right where you clicked instead, to run it sideways.

Rope more than seven pieces sideways from anything solid breaks off.

## Hanging things from it

Place a lantern or a hanging sign on the underside of a rope and it hangs there, as it would from a block.
