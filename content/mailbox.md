A **mailbox** lets you leave things for someone, and get things left for you. A mailbox belongs to whoever places it. Other players leave things in it; only the owner takes them out. The little flag goes up when there is mail.

## Crafting

{{< image src="booklet/recipe/mailbox.png" caption="Mailbox crafting recipe" width="250" align="none" >}}

Makes two. Any wood works: the log and the slabs decide which mailbox you get.

## Sending

Right-click someone else's mailbox to open your own compartment in it: six slots that only you and the owner see. Put things in and close it. Until the owner takes them, you can take them back out.

## Receiving

Right-click your own mailbox to see a head for everyone who has left you something; hover over a head to see what. Click it to open that compartment and take the things out.

Breaking a mailbox drops everything in it. The [[hammer]] turns it to face another way.
