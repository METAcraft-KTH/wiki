**The Nether** is a hellish dimension reached through a nether portal. It is the main source of quartz, glowstone, and blaze rods. See also the [official site](https://example.com).

## Biomes

- Nether Wastes
- Crimson Forest
- Soul Sand Valley

## Structures

Chests in nether fortresses may contain an [Iron Sword](/weapons/iron-sword).
