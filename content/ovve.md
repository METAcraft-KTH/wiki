An **ovve** is your student overall: one garment, worn in the legs slot, that you sew patches onto. It works whether or not it is nercabbad.

{{< image src="ovvar_inventory.png" caption="The ovve in the inventory" width="300" >}}
{{< image src="ovve.png" caption="An ovve" width="150" >}}

## Patches

You can earn patches at chapter events and in games. A patch you are given goes into your stash with a flourish and a line in chat, and waits there until you take it out.

Open the stash with `/ovvar patches` and hover a patch to see who drew it and where it may be sewn.

## Storage

The ovve is also a bundle, with double the usual capacity.
