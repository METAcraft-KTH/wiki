An **ovve** is your student overall: one garment, worn in the legs slot, that you sew [[patches]] onto. It works whether or not it is nercabbad.

{{< image src="ovvar_inventory.png" caption="The ovve in the inventory" width="300" >}}

## Variants

* Data Ovve
* IT Ovve
* IT Kisel Ovve
* Media Frack

## Obtaining

Each variant can be bought at spawn for 32 iron ingots. This is a limited-time offer that disappears on November 2nd.

Ovvar can also be crafted with chapter-colored wool.

## Ownership

The first player to wear a new ovve or frack becomes its owner, and it stays locked to them.

## Patches

{{< main "Patches" >}}

Place your ovve on an armor stand to sew [[patches]] onto it. Your patch stash is synced across METAcraft's event and survival servers.

## Storage

The ovve is also a bundle, with double the usual capacity.
