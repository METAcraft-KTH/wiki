An **ovve** is your student overall: one garment, worn in the legs slot, that you sew [[patches]] onto. What you sew on stays yours on every server, and only you can change it. It works whether or not it is nercabbad.

{{< image src="ovvar_inventory.png" caption="The ovve in the inventory" width="300" >}}

## Variants

{{< image src="booklet/ovvar/chapters.png" caption="One ovve per chapter" width="300" >}}

One ovve per chapter:

* {{< sprite "ovvar/icon_data.png" "Data Ovve" >}} **Data Ovve**, in cerise
* {{< sprite "ovvar/icon_it.png" "IT Ovve" >}} **IT Ovve**, in purple
* {{< sprite "ovvar/icon_it_kisel.png" "IT Kisel Ovve" >}} **IT Kisel Ovve**, the older IT ovve
* {{< sprite "ovvar/icon_mediafrack.png" "Media Frack" >}} **Media Frack**, a tailcoat

Each ovve is a single item that goes in the **legs** slot: drag it in, or shift-click it. Right-clicking does not equip it (see [Storage](#storage)). It protects like leather and never wears out.

The **Media Frack** is a tailcoat, so it goes in the **chest** slot instead. It is its own top: there is nothing to roll up or down, and patches go only on the coat. Its pockets work like an ovve's.

## Obtaining

Each variant can be bought at spawn for 32 iron ingots. This is a limited-time offer that disappears on November 2nd.

Ovvar can also be crafted with chapter-colored wool.

## The top

Sneak and right-click the ovve to roll its top **up** or **down**. With the top up and nothing in your chest slot the sleeves show; a chestplate goes on over it as usual and hides the top until it comes off again. The top is not a separate item: it comes and goes with the ovve.

## Storage

The ovve is also a bundle. Click items onto it to fill it, in your inventory or straight onto its slot while you wear it; right-click and hold to empty it. It holds twice what a bundle does.

## Ownership

An ovve becomes the property of the first player to pick it up. Somebody else's ovve will not go on you and nobody but you can sew on yours, so an ovve can be traded, but the design on it is its owner's.

What you have sewn on is stored once for all the servers. Sew on one server, log into another wearing the same ovve; lose the item and a new ovve of that chapter shows your design again. Pockets and enchantments stay with the item; the look and the patches travel.

## Patches

{{< main "Patches" >}}

Place your ovve on an armor stand to sew [[patches]] onto it.

## Commands

Command | Effect
--- | ---
`/ovvar stash` | Opens your wardrobe and patch stash
`/ovvar look [player]` | Shows somebody's ovve as they have it, online or not; on its own it shows yours
`/ovvar reload` | Fetches the latest resource pack, when somebody's patches look out of date
`/ovvar shaders` | Links the shader patcher, if you play with a shaderpack (see [[shaders]])
