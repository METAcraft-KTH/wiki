The **hammer** turns blocks round without breaking them.

## Crafting

{{< image src="booklet/recipe/hammer.png" caption="Hammer crafting recipe" width="250" align="none" >}}

## Usage

Right-click or hit a placed block with it to turn it, without breaking it:

* logs and other pillars: which way they lie;
* slabs: top half or bottom half;
* stairs: click the flat step to turn them, the full-width half to flip them upside down, the narrow half to change their corner shape;
* trapdoors, fence gates and [[mailbox|mailboxes]]: which way they face;
* bamboo: its leaves;
* [[sign post|sign posts]]: a sign 15° at a time.

It only works where you are allowed to build. See also the [[trowel]].
