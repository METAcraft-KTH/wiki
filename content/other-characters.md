This is a list of minor characters who have existed in METAcraft's various game worlds.

## Season 3

### METAplace

{{< main "METAplace" >}}

#### Sir Doctor Nicole David von Ludwig XVIII

Lead researcher at White Mesa, a research institute that, among other things, supplied new blocks for the players to place during [[METAplace]]. When a coordinated chicken attack destroyed White Mesa between day 4 and day 5 of the event, he died in the chaos. He never appeared in the game.

#### White Mesa Delivery Man

A villager who flew past the canvas at random times from day 2 onwards. He was the only way to get concrete powder and wool. After White Mesa was destroyed on day 5, he changed his name to "Homeless Delivery Man".
