The **world border** limits the playable area of the Season 6 SMP. It started out at 1000×1000 blocks.

## Expanding

The border grows as players donate gold blocks to [[Gold Gary Jr.]] at spawn. Every gold block adds 1 block to the border's diameter.

There is a daily limit on how many blocks each player can donate, and a cap on how wide the border can get.
