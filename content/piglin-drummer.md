The **Piglin Drummer** is a piglin with a war drum. It leads every wave of a [[Rift Raids|Rift Raid]] from the second one on.

## Tells

It never swings at you, and it keeps its distance while it drums. You hear it before you see it: when it sees a player within 16 blocks, it lets out a war cry and drums a beat every half second.

## The big beat

Every fourth beat it stops, raises its arms with a roar, and slams the drum in a ring of flame.

* Piglins within 12 blocks get Speed and Strength for 6 seconds.
* Players within 4 blocks take 2 hearts of damage and are thrown back.

## Weaknesses

Every drummer keeps time with the world clock, so they all beat and slam together, and the beat never changes. Hit between slams, and step back when the arms go up. It has 30 health and can wear armor. Kill it first: no drummer, no buffs.

## In the Nether

Now and then a drummer walks with the Nether's own piglins: about 3 in every 100 piglins that spawn there bring one. It drums when its piglins fight and buffs them. Like them, it may wear gold armor, its big beat spares players who wear gold, and it turns zombie in the Overworld. Hit it and the piglins near it turn on you.

A rift's drummer never turns zombie, and gold never spares you from a rift raid.

## Chained Ghast

A drummer also rides the deck of every [[Chained Ghast]]. Its big beat buffs the piglins on the deck and on the ground below, but it can't knock you back from up there.
