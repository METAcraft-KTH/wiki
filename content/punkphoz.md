**Punkphöz** is a wandering trader who turns up now and then near players, alongside the usual wandering traders (he never replaces one). He is the only way to get the newer chapters' [[ovve|ovvar]] in survival. He replaced the [[Phoz|Phöz]] at spawn.

## Trades

What he sells is picked anew each visit, for emeralds:

* **Ovvar**: a few chapters' ovvar or fracks each visit, from the chapters nobody else sells. A chapter he offered recently is less likely to come straight back.
* **[[Patches]]**: a couple of the patches found in chests. Never a crafted patch, nor one that is only given out.
* **[[Banner patterns|Banner pattern items]]**: the ITK, QMISK, TMEIT and Pirkko patterns.
* Sometimes the **Curse of the Spilled Drink**, as an enchanted book (see [ovve enchantments](/ovve/#enchantments)).
