METAcraft typically uses custom worldgen datapacks. They give the world a more unique feel without affecting gameplay or progression.

{{< image src="overworld.png" caption="The Overworld at sunset" width="350" >}}

## Overworld

The Overworld uses the datapacks [Geophilic](https://modrinth.com/datapack/geophilic), [Tectonic](https://modrinth.com/datapack/tectonic) and [Structory](https://modrinth.com/datapack/structory) with some private changes. On average, the biomes are 2.7x bigger than vanilla's biomes, with much taller mountains and slopes.

The border is set to be 1000 x 1000 blocks at launch day and gradually expands in the first few weeks of the season.

## Nether

**The Nether's world generation now goes up to Y=255**, meaning the Nether roof is effectively patched. For technical players, Bedrock is now considered non-solid blocks and will not affect mob spawn rates when building below it. See [[technical changes]].
