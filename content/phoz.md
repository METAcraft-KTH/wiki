The **Phöz** was a colloquial name for the three NPCs at spawn. They offered limited-time trade offers for [[ovve|ovves and fracks]]. They were removed in [Museum Showdown 2026](/patch-notes/#museum-showdown-2026); ovvar are now sold by the wandering [[Punkphoz|Punkphöz]].

## Trading

The Phöz had four trades: 32 iron ingots for 1 of the four ovve variants.
