The **trowel** lays blocks for you, at random.

## Crafting

{{< image src="booklet/recipe/trowel.png" caption="Trowel crafting recipe" width="250" align="none" >}}

## Usage

Right-click with it and it places a random block from your hotbar. Put cobblestone, mossy cobblestone and gravel there and a path comes out looking worn in.

Every hotbar slot with a block in it is as likely as the next, so put a block in more slots to get more of it. Keep your off hand free of blocks: with a block there, the trowel does nothing.

It only works where you are allowed to build. See also the [[hammer]].
