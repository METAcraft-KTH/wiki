**Borgarorden** ("the Burgher Order") was the name of the group that worked on METAcraft during [[Season 2]] and [[Season 3]]. They also played a part in both seasons' stories, often as a protective power over the game world.

In [[Season 4]] the name "the METAcraft group" is used instead, and the group no longer has any part in the story.
