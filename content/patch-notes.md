## Season 6 Opening
26 October 2026

* Added Cerise Dye, Laserviolet Dye, and RGB Dye (see [[dyes]])
  * They can only be obtained from their respective flowers
  * They can be used to dye and color everything normal dyes can
  * RGB stained glass cannot be used to color beacon beams
* Added [[Cerose]], [[Laser Violet]] and [[RGBouquet]]
  * These flowers can only be obtained from Sniffers and are used to craft the dyes mentioned above
* Added [[Pirkko|Pirkkos]]
  * They spawn as small, weak, slow-moving hostile entities mainly found in caves
  * When killed, it drops itself as an item and can be placed as a decorative block
  * They exist in 19 colors: the vanilla 16 colors + the 3 modded colors above
  * Pirkko items cannot be crafted or dyed
* Added Data Ovve, IT Ovve, IT Kisel Ovve and Media Frack (see [[ovve]])
  * The first player to equip a new ovve or frack will become its owner
  * It is functionally equivalent to a double-capacity bundle but is worn in the legs slot
  * They can be purchased at spawn for 32 iron ingots each
  * This is a limited-time offer that will disappear on November 2nd
* Added 15(?) [[patches]]
  * They can be sewn onto an ovve or frack when it is placed on an armor stand
* Added [[Rift Raids]]
  * Attempting to open a Nether portal will instead start a Rift Raid
  * The bigger the Nether portal frame, the more difficult the raid will be
  * Piglins will appear from the portal, and killing them yields Nether loot
  * After the raid ends, the obsidian portal frame is converted to crying obsidian
* Added [[Piglin Grenadier]], [[Piglin Drummer]], [[Chained Ghast]], [[Piglin General]]
  * These mobs only spawn during Rift Raids
* Added a [[world border]] that constrains the playable area to 1000x1000
