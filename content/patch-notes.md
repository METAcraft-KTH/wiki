## Museum Showdown 2026
- Museum Showdown 2026 begins
  - This is a team-based collection event that lasts from week 47 (16 Nov) to week 49 (30 Nov), and you represent either East, West or South
  - The objective is to submit as many unique items as possible while the event is running
    - "Unique item" is based on item ID, so two differently enchanted diamond swords do not count
  - After the event ends, the top 5 players who have submitted the most amount of unique items earn one-of-its-kind hats
  - The team with the most amount of unique items submitted also wins a team prize
  - Which items have been submitted by which player/team can be found on the website
- Added new Item Collector NPC at spawn
  - Talk to him while holding an item in your main hand to mark it as submitted, and have it count towards your person and your team
- Removed Phöz at spawn
- Added Punkphöz
  - Spawns randomly near players similarly to wandering traders, selling ovves and banner patterns
- Added a significant amount of ovves in different colors from different chapters
  - They must be purchased from Punkphöz
- Added 20 new banner and shield patterns based on THS chapter logos
  - These can be applied in looms directly without any templates
- Added a few banner and shield patterns based on chapter committees
  - Templates must be purchased from Punkphöz
- Zombies can now spawn wearing ovves, and killing them drops a random patch
- Added several ovve enchantments that make them significantly more useful and viable in gameplay and as armor
  - They are accessed by simply placing your ovve in an enchanting table
- Players and mobs can no longer trample farmland

## Bigger Bundles, Bigger Bets
* Added [[item skins|hats]]
  * A hat is a visual-only reskin for helmets
  * A helmet that has a hat applied can sometimes be dyed the same way leather helmets can
  * If you have unlocked a hat skin, use `/itemskins` to apply the hat to a helmet in your inventory
* Added skin tokens
  * A 1-time use consumable that unlocks a certain reskin cosmetic for the user
  * Already unlocked hats can be un-unlocked and returned to a Skin Token
* Added lootboxes
  * It is a guaranteed drop from hat-wearing mobs that rarely spawn in the Overworld
    * The less a chunk is explored, the higher the spawn rate
  * A lootbox contains a random Skin Token for a hat cosmetic
  * This is the only way to obtain tokens and unlock hats at the moment
* Bundles are now [[bundle upgrades|upgradable]]
  * See the linked wiki page for recipes
* Added `/chapter`
  * Use it to color your name tag with your chapter's name
* The maximum border cap has been removed
* Every gold block donated now increase the Oveworld diameter by 2 blocks (from 1) and Nether by 1 block (from 0.5)
* Fixed sign post edit gui closing itself immediately when opened

## Season 6 Opening
26 October 2026

* Added Cerise Dye, Laserviolet Dye, and RGB Dye (see [[dyes]])
  * They can only be obtained from their respective flowers
  * They can be used to dye and color everything normal dyes can
  * RGB stained glass cannot be used to color beacon beams
* Added [[Cerose]], [[Laser Violet]] and [[RGBouquet]]
  * These flowers can only be obtained from Sniffers and are used to craft the dyes mentioned above
* Added [[Pirkko|Pirkkos]]
  * They spawn as small, weak, slow-moving hostile entities mainly found in caves
  * When killed, it drops itself as an item and can be placed as a decorative block
  * They exist in 19 colors: the vanilla 16 colors + the 3 modded colors above
  * Pirkko items cannot be crafted or dyed
* Added Data Ovve, IT Ovve, IT Kisel Ovve and Media Frack (see [[ovve]])
  * The first player to equip a new ovve or frack will become its owner
  * It is functionally equivalent to a double-capacity bundle but is worn in the legs slot
  * They can be purchased at spawn for 32 iron ingots each
  * This is a limited-time offer that will disappear on November 2nd
* Added 15(?) [[patches]]
  * They can be sewn onto an ovve or frack when it is placed on an armor stand
* Added [[Rift Raids]]
  * Attempting to open a Nether portal will instead start a Rift Raid
  * The bigger the Nether portal frame, the more difficult the raid will be
  * Piglins will appear from the portal, and killing them yields Nether loot
  * After the raid ends, the obsidian portal frame is converted to crying obsidian
* Added [[Piglin Grenadier]], [[Piglin Drummer]], [[Chained Ghast]], [[Piglin General]]
  * These mobs only spawn during Rift Raids
* Added a [[world border]] that constrains the playable area to 1000x1000
