**Season 3** was an iteration of METAcraft's survival server that ran during the spring term of 2024. The term may also loosely refer to everything Minecraft-related that happened during the 2023/24 academic year, particularly in the autumn term of 2023.

## Plot

Long ago there lived a person of great power, and his name was [[beso|Be$o$]]. He had taken control of practically the whole world, but then the burgher council had had enough. They invented a weapon stronger than anything else, and used it to annihilate Be$o$. Thousands of years later, a meteorite falls from the sky. It is taken to a secret laboratory beneath what, long ago, was a subway just outside Campus. Nobody knew exactly what the object was, but it seemed to have magical powers.

The walls between the dimensions had hardened, so it was impossible to travel to the ancient so-called Nether dimension. But one day Professor Alpert Horten and his assistants managed to open the way, and all sorts of creatures came out through the portal. The professor, feeling guilty for everything that had happened, ran into the portal and disappeared.

Some time later, Professor Horten managed to make contact with his assistants and send them some inventions he had come up with.

But the unrest grew worse and worse. Assistant 18 became more and more aggressive and fired people over trifles. It also felt like everyone was getting more and more tired. But the remaining assistants managed to open the link to yet another ancient dimension, the End dimension. Once there, an epic battle against the legendary Ender Dragon followed.

When the dragon was defeated and everyone went through the exit portal, they ended up in a mysterious dimension. There was Amazon, and the ghostly remains of the legendary Be$o$. It was an even fight, but Be$o$ was not strong enough to defeat his opponents. Just as Be$o$ was about to die, something happened that nobody could have foreseen: Be$o$ turned into the object.

The object was practically immortal. The brave heroes who had defeated Be$o$ stood no chance. The assistants argued, but finally suggested that a mixture of resonite, gunpowder, ender pearl and poisonous potato might work as a weapon. The heroes tried it, and it weakened the object, but then something terrifying happened. The heroes' ability to respawn after death was taken from them, and they began to fall one by one. The assistants wished the professor were there to help them. Then Assistant 16 had an idea. This assistant had invented a way to link DNA to ender pearls, and suggested using it to bring Professor Alpert Horten home. Assistant 18 would hear none of it and fired Assistant 16. But Assistant 16 tried anyway, and the professor came back. The professor suggested that, since the object had been weakened, one could travel into it and wish for one's body back, and suddenly those who had fallen managed to come back. The object did everything in its power to survive, even creating Withers towards the end. But its days were numbered, and with every blow it grew smaller and smaller. Finally the object stopped moving: the heroes had won. The professor suggested they pick the object up in a bucket and give it to him, which they did. The professor could then use it to open a portal back to Campus. But he had been hit by one of the object's attacks during the battle and was not well. In the end the professor died of radiation poisoning. Assistant 18 then took the object and took over the research. What happened to the object after that is unknown.

## Technical

### Verification

Unlike the previous season, which used whitelists, Season 3 introduced a new verification system: you verified yourself as a KTH student with a verification code sent to your KTH email address.

Plus-ones were added by having the KTH student and the plus-one logged in to the server at the same time; the student could then type a special command to add the player.

### Mods and plugins

[[Simple Voice Chat]] was installed on the survival server, and players were encouraged to install the mod client-side to use voice chat in game. *(TODO: write about the plugins)*

### Hosting

All of METAcraft's servers that academic year were hosted on an EX44 dedicated server at Hetzner in Helsinki.

## Administration

Season 3 was the first and last time METAcraft was an independent project within Datasektionen. The officer position for METAcraft's project lead was called "[[Herobrine]]".

Season 3 had the largest project group in METAcraft's history, with 30 members including Herobrine.
