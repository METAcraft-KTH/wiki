The **Copper Golem** is a small neutral mob that wanders around presses copper buttons at random.

## Spawning

A copper golem is created by placing a carved pumpkin on top of a copper block.

## Behavior

Copper golems ignore players unless attacked. Over time they oxidize and eventually freeze into statues.

### Oxidation

| Stage | Speed |
|---|---|
| Unaffected | 100% |
| Exposed | 75% |
| Weathered | 50% |
| Oxidized | 0% (statue) |

## Combat

An [Iron Sword](iron-sword) kills one in seven hits.
