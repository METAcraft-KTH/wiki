A **mystifier** is an upgrade template that can be applied to a melee weapon to give it **mystic** quality. A mystic weapon counts the mobs killed with it, if they belong to a chosen category.

It was added to the server on 15 October 2024, during the update and PVP event "[[a-new-continent|A New Continent...?]]".

## Obtaining

Mystifiers could only be obtained by opening a [[Loot box|loot box]], or bought directly from [[Sleven]] for 32 [[certificate|certificates]].

During the event that introduced them, [[Soul Bottle|soul bottles]] could be traded to the NPC "Soul Merchant" for a chance at a mystifier.

## Usage

{{< image src="history/mystifier_smithing_table.png" caption="Put the mystifier in a [smithing table](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Smithing_Table) with an ingredient to apply it to a melee weapon." width="300" >}}

A mystifier can only be applied to melee weapons: swords, axes and [maces](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mace).

To upgrade a weapon, place the weapon, the mystifier and an ingredient in a [smithing table](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Smithing_Table). The ingredient decides which category of mobs the weapon counts kills of:

Ingredient | Mob category
--- | ---
Rotten Flesh | Undead (zombies, skeletons, etc.)
Coal | Wither skeletons only
[Ominous Bottle](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ominous_Bottle) | [Raiders](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Raid)
Diamond | Players

A weapon can only have one mob category. Applying a new mystifier changes the category, but resets the count to zero.
