**Player shops** let you sell to other players from a barrel. They trade with it as with a villager, even while you are away.

{{< image src="shop_crafting.jpg" caption="Shop block crafting recipe (any color of planks and wool)" width="300" >}}

## Setting up

Craft a shop from 3 wool across the top, a barrel between two planks in the middle, and an iron ingot between two planks at the bottom. Place it on top of a **barrel** or on its side: one shop to a barrel. Fill the barrel with what you sell.

## Your trades

Use your shop to set its trades (sneak to see it as a buyer does): up to 9, each a row of three slots. **1** and **2** are the price (the second is optional), **R** is what the buyer gets.

* Click a slot with an item to put it there: left-click for the whole stack, right-click for one.
* With an empty hand, left-click adds one and right-click takes one away. Hold shift to add or take a stack.

The trades are saved when you close the menu.

## Buying

Anyone else who uses the shop, or its barrel, gets a trading menu like a villager's. A trade works while the barrel holds what it sells and has room for the payment, which goes into the barrel. Only you can open the barrel itself.

* A hopper under the barrel takes the payments instead.
* Hoppers pointing into the barrel add their items to the stock.
* No hopper can put items into the barrel or take them out.

## Shop keys

Use your shop with a gold ingot in hand and the ingot becomes a key to it. Whoever holds the key, in either hand, can change the trades and open the barrel as you can.

## Moving a shop

Break the shop and it keeps its trades: place it on another barrel and they are back. To wipe them, put it through a grindstone.
