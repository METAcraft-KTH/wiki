**Player shops** let you set up your own shops with the shop block, crafted from planks and wool (any color of each).

{{< image src="shop_crafting.jpg" caption="Shop block crafting recipe (any color of planks and wool)" width="300" >}}

## Setup

The shop block must be placed on top of a **barrel** that stores input/output. Once placed, right-click the shop block to start configuring your trades.
