The **Wrench** makes block entities such as chests, furnaces and dispensers movable. Right-click a block entity with the wrench to pick it up.

## History

{{< image src="history/wrench_usage.gif" caption="Using the wrench in Season 4" width="300" >}}

The wrench was first added in [[Season 4]], on 23 February 2025, day 6 of [[A Week of Updates]]. It was crafted the same way, and its texture was taken from the [Reimagined](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/reimagined) resource pack.

It worked differently back then: right-clicking a block entity toggled whether pistons could move it. Right-clicking the air set the default for the blocks you placed from then on, for example so that every chest you placed afterwards could be moved by pistons.
