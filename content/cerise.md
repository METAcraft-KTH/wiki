**Cerise** (`#E83D84`) is the primary color of Konglig Datasektionen. METAcraft's servers have added it by replacing an existing color: in [[Season 3]] that color was magenta, but in [[Season 4]] it was changed to pink.

Today Cerise is a dye of its own and replaces nothing: see [[dyes]].
