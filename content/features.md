The world of METAcraft has many new exciting features compared to the usual, vanilla Minecraft experience. You play METAcraft on a vanilla client: there is nothing to install, and the server sends its own resource pack when you join. This page will list all of them, but a select few are hidden unannounced features that you will need to discover on your own by playing.

In game, `/guide` opens the METAcraft booklet, which describes most of these features.

This page only lists **additions**, but there are also several vanilla things we've tweaked. You should read through the [[technical changes]].

# Vanilla+

Additions that feel like they could be vanilla, and fit in with the blocks and items you know.

## Recipes

Type `/polydex` to open a list of every item on the server, ours included. Click an item to see how it is made; right-click it to see what it is used in. To find something by name: `/polydex search` and a word, like `/polydex search patch`.

Some things are not made at a crafting table, and Polydex can't show them: sewing a patch on an ovve, [[brewing]] in a cauldron, or concrete in a cauldron.

Polydex also shows the name of the block or creature you are looking at, and which mod it comes from, at the top of the screen. To change where it shows, or turn it off: `/polydex hover style` with `polydex:sidebar`, `polydex:bossbar` or `polydex:disabled`.

## Concrete crafting

{{< image src="booklet/qol/concrete.png" caption="Concrete in a cauldron" width="200" >}}

Throw concrete powder into a cauldron with water in it and it turns into concrete. The whole stack you threw changes, for one level of water, so throw full stacks: 64 powder costs the same water as one.

Every color works. A full cauldron does three stacks. Fill it again with a water bucket, or let rain or dripping dripstone do it. A hopper under the cauldron picks up the concrete.

## New decor

{{< image src="booklet/decorating/corner.png" caption="New decor" width="250" >}}

A few extras for your builds, all in the look of the blocks you know, and all crafted in survival:

* [[Canvas]]: paint pixel art and hang it up
* [[Mailbox|Mailboxes]]: leave things for someone, and get things left for you
* [[Rope]]: hang it from anything solid, stretch it across a gap
* [[Sign Post|Sign posts]]: arrow signs on a fence post, pointing the way
* [[Hammer]]: turn blocks without breaking them
* [[Trowel]]: lay blocks at random from your hotbar

### Lanterns on walls

{{< image src="booklet/decorating/lantern.png" caption="A lantern on a wall" width="150" >}}

**Sneak** and right-click the side of a block with a lantern to hang it on the wall, on a bracket. Fences and walls take them too. Every lantern works: plain, soul and copper.

Where a lantern would have nothing to stand on or hang from, clicking the side of a block puts it on the wall even without sneaking.

### Leads between fences

{{< image src="booklet/decorating/lead.png" caption="A lead between fences" width="150" >}}

Right-click a fence with a lead to tie one end to it, then right-click another fence to tie the other end. No animal needed: a washing line, a railing, a line for bunting. Hit either knot to take the lead down again.

## Gestures

Type `/gesture` to open the gestures menu, and pick one: wave, dab, facepalm and more. Your character plays it for everyone near you, and you watch from outside, as they see it. Some gestures play a song.

You have to stand on the ground to start one. It ends by itself, or sneak to stop it early. The menu is also in your Quick Actions.

## Armor stand statues

Armor stands can be configured into different poses! More info on how to do it will arrive soon.

## Custom heads

Players always drop their head when killed by another player, and all mobs have a chance to drop their head when killed by a player. [See exact probabilities here.](https://wiki.vanillatweaks.net/More_Mob_Heads)

**Wandering Traders** will also sell custom mini-blocks you can use to decorate your builds.

For additional custom heads, there is a database of heads that you will be able to access by trading in mob heads. More information on this soon.
