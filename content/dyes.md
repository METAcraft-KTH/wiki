Three new dyes have been added to the game: **Cerise**, **Laser Violet** and **RGB**, an animated dye representing the Media chapter. They can be used on all the blocks that other dyes can, including but not limited to wool, glass, concrete, beds, glazed terracottas, leather armor and candles.

RGB stained glass cannot be used to color beacon beams.

## Obtaining

Each dye can only be crafted from its matching flower, and the flowers can only be acquired from Sniffers:

Dye | Flower
--- | ---
Cerise Dye | [[Cerose]]
Laser Violet Dye | [[Laser Violet]]
RGB Dye | [[RGBouquet]]
