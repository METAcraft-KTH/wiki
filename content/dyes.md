Three new dyes have been added to the game: **Cerise**, **Laser Violet** and a secret animated dye representing the Media chapter. They can be used on all the blocks that other dyes can, including but not limited to wool, glass, concrete, beds, glazed terracottas, leather armor and candles.

## Obtaining

The dyes are obtained from matching flowers, which can only be acquired from Sniffers.
