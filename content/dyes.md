Three new dyes have been added to the game: **Cerise**, **Laser Violet** and **RGB**, an animated dye representing the Media chapter. They work like any dye, on almost everything a dye works on. RGB never holds still: what you dye with it cycles through the rainbow.

{{< image src="booklet/moredyes/colors.png" caption="The three colors" width="300" >}}

## Obtaining

Each dye can only be crafted from its matching flower. One flower makes one dye; plant or pot the rest.

Dye | Flower
--- | ---
Cerise Dye | [[Cerose]]
Laser Violet Dye | [[Laser Violet]]
RGB Dye | [[RGBouquet]]

The flowers can only be acquired from **Sniffers**. A sniffer digging in grass or dirt sometimes turns up one of them, each as often as its own torchflower seeds and pitcher pods. Sniffers hatch from a sniffer egg, found in the suspicious sand of warm ocean ruins.

{{< image src="booklet/recipe/cerise_dye.png" caption="Cerise Dye" width="250" align="none" >}}

## What they color

* Wool, carpet, beds, candles, banners and shields
* Terracotta, glazed terracotta, concrete and concrete powder
* Stained glass and panes, shulker boxes, bundles
* Wool and concrete stairs and slabs, cushions
* A happy ghast's harness, a llama's carpet
* Sheep: use the dye on one, shear it for wool in the color; two of a color have lambs in it
* Leather armor, leather horse armor, wolf armor and firework stars, mixed with other dyes as usual
* Sign text, in exactly the color; editing the sign keeps it
* A [[canvas]], in the nearest color a canvas has
* A pet's collar, in the nearest vanilla color

A beacon beam goes through Cerise, Laser Violet and RGB stained glass uncolored.

## RGB

Blocks, banners and dyed armor in RGB cycle through the rainbow. Dye armor with RGB on its own: mixed with other dyes it won't take, and a plain dye on RGB armor replaces it. A happy ghast's harness and a llama's carpet wear red, green and blue stripes, and a canvas gets confetti in all its colors. Where a color can't move, RGB is red: fireworks, sign text and maps.

## Banners and shields

{{< image src="booklet/recipe/cerise_banner.png" caption="Cerise Banner" width="250" align="none" >}}

Six Cerise Wool over a stick make a **Cerise Banner**, the way six white wool make a white one; six Laser Violet or RGB Wool make a Laser Violet or RGB one. At a loom it takes six patterns on top of its color, like any banner; with five on it, the loom's banner slot shows it in the nearest vanilla color, and the banner that comes out is still its own color. It decorates a shield and copies onto a plain white banner. A cauldron washes its patterns off, but not its color.

All three dyes go in a loom like any dye, on any banner: pick a pattern and it comes out in that color. The pattern buttons name the nearest vanilla color, and the preview can show that color for a moment. Patterns that need a pattern item need it in the loom, as usual. See also [[banner patterns]].
