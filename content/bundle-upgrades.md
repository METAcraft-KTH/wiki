**Bundle upgrades** increase how much a bundle can hold. They were added in the [[patch-notes|6.2 Bundle Update]].

## Tiers

| Tier | Capacity |
|------|----------|
| 1 (normal bundle) | 1 stack (64 items) |
| 2 | 2 stacks (128 items) |
| 3 | 3 stacks (192 items) |
