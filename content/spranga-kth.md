**Spränga KTH** ("Blow Up KTH") is a song by Twizzy Rude. A music disc that plays the song exists as an item on METAcraft's survival server.

It was added on 15 October 2024, during the update and PVP event "[[a-new-continent|A New Continent...?]]".

You can listen to the song [on SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/twizzy-rude/spranga-kth).

## Obtaining

The music disc could only be obtained by opening a [[Loot box|loot box]], which could be bought at [[Sleven]].
