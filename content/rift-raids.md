A **Rift Raid** is a raid that starts when you try to open a Nether portal. Instead of opening a way to the Nether, the portal tears open a rift and the Nether comes to you.

## Starting a raid

Build a Nether portal frame and light it, as you normally would. The frame can face any direction.

The bigger the portal frame, the more difficult the raid will be.

## The raid

Piglins pour out of the portal. Killing them yields Nether loot, so raids are how you get Nether resources.

Some mobs only ever appear during Rift Raids:

* [[Bombardier]]
* [[Drummer]]
* [[Chained Ghast]]
* [[General]]

## Aftermath

When the raid ends, the obsidian portal frame is converted to crying obsidian.
