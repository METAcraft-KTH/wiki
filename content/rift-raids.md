A **Rift Raid** is a piglin raid that starts when you tear open a rift. The Nether is held back at first: for now a lit Nether portal takes you nowhere and opens a **rift** instead. The realm will stabilise in time, and the Nether opens.

## Lighting a rift

A rift is lit three times before it tears:

* **First light:** light a Nether portal frame in the Overworld and a rift opens. The portal flickers but will not hold, and fine cracks run across it. Everyone within 32 blocks is warned in chat.
* **Second light:** use flint and steel or a fire charge on the rift or its frame. It strains and its cracks spread, one light from tearing. Leave it for 5 minutes and it settles back.
* **Third light:** it tears open, reddening, with the Nether showing through. A horn sounds, and the first wave comes 10 seconds later.

Break a frame block and a quiet rift fades.

The realm can only take 4 torn rifts at once. A third light is refused until one closes.

## Sizes

A rift's size is the number of blocks inside its frame. A plain portal is 6 blocks, so it is small. Portals can be any size and shape (see [[technical changes]]).

Size | Blocks inside frame | Waves
--- | --- | ---
Small | up to 20 | 3
Medium | 21 to 60 | 5
Large | 61 or more | 7

In large rifts, [[Chained Ghast|Chained Ghasts]] come in waves 5 and 7, and the [[Piglin General]] in the last.

## The raid

Everyone within 48 blocks takes part. Each player after the first brings a quarter more piglins and hoglins, up to twice as many. A boss bar counts down the wave; the next one comes when the last mob is dead. After 90 seconds, the stragglers glow.

Rift mobs that stray more than 64 blocks are pulled back to the rift. Gold won't help you: rift piglins attack players in gold armor and never barter.

If nobody stays within 48 blocks for 3 minutes, the rift collapses with no reward. Breaking a frame block collapses a raiding rift too.

## Who comes through

Crossbow piglins, spear piglins and hoglins make up most of a wave. Later waves add ghasts with a crossbow piglin on board, striders with crossbow piglins riding them, and brutes with golden axes. From wave 2, a [[Piglin Drummer]] leads each wave.

Some mobs only ever appear during Rift Raids:

* [[Piglin Drummer]]
* [[Piglin Grenadier]]
* [[Chained Ghast]]
* [[Piglin General]]

Rift mobs bring the Nether with them:

* A rift ghast shoots at players above and below it, not only level with it. When it dies, its rider glides down.
* A rift strider stays warm off lava, so it never slows down, and rain doesn't hurt it. Water still does.
* No rift piglin or hoglin turns zombie in the Overworld.
* You can't trap rift mobs in boats or minecarts.

## Spoils

Rift piglins only drop loot when a player kills them, and no rift mob drops its gear.

Mob | Drops
--- | ---
Piglins | Up to 2 quartz and 3 gold nuggets, often glowstone dust, sometimes nether wart
Brutes | The same, and always a blaze rod
[[Piglin Drummer\|Piglin Drummers]] | The same, and always a gold ingot
Hoglins, ghasts, striders | Their usual drops
[[Piglin General]] | See its page

## Winning

Win the last wave and the rift bursts into gold ingots and glowstone dust:

Size | Gold ingots | Glowstone dust
--- | --- | ---
Small | 3 to 5 | 4 to 8
Medium | 6 to 10 | 8 to 16
Large | 12 to 18 | 16 to 24

## Aftermath

When a torn rift closes, won or lost, its obsidian frame turns to crying obsidian. Keep it.

## Stabilising

When the realm steadies, waiting rifts turn into working Nether portals, new portals light as normal, and raids still running finish as usual.

{{< image src="booklet/rifts/crying_frame.png" caption="A crying obsidian frame" width="250" >}}

After that, a frame of crying obsidian in the Overworld opens a rift on purpose. Light it with flint and steel or a fire charge, and the three lights, sizes and spoils work as before. Any closed shape works, up to 21 blocks wide and tall. Each crying-obsidian frame gives one raid, then crumbles away.

When the Nether opens, it will be amplified (see [[world generation]]).
