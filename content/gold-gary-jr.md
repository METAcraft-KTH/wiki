**Gold Gary Jr.** sits at spawn and waits for players to feed him gold blocks.

## Expanding the border

Every gold block donated to Gold Gary Jr. adds 1 block to the diameter of the [[world border]].

When he was added, each player could donate at most 10 blocks per day, and the border could grow to at most 1250 blocks wide. These limits may change.
