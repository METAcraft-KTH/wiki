The chapters' own **banner patterns** go on banners the way vanilla's do: in a loom, in any dye color, Cerise, Laser Violet and RGB included (see [[dyes]]). Craft the banner onto a shield and the shield shows it too.

## Chapter patterns

Every chapter has a pattern of its own: its logo. Chapter patterns need no pattern item. Put a banner and a dye in any loom and pick your chapter from the list.

## The IT clubs and Pirkko

**ITK**, **QMISK**, **TMEIT** and **[[Pirkko]]** each need their pattern item in the loom, like the flower or creeper pattern: the ITK, QMISK, TMEIT or Pirkko Banner Pattern. The item is not used up.

The pattern items can't be found in chests. [[Punkphoz|Punkphöz]], a wandering trader who turns up now and then, sells them.

## The painting

Hang a painting on a wall with room for one 3 wide and 4 high and you may get IT's own: **The Guardian of Kistan**, by Emelie Stark.
