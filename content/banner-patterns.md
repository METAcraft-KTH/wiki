The chapters' own **banner patterns** go on banners the way vanilla's do: in a loom, in any dye color, Cerise, Laser Violet and RGB included (see [[dyes]]). Craft the banner onto a shield and the shield shows it too.

## In any loom

**ITK**, **QMISK** and **TMEIT** need no pattern item. Put a banner and a dye in any loom and pick them from the list.

## Pirkko

The **[[Pirkko]]** pattern needs its pattern item, the Pirkko Banner Pattern, in the loom, like the flower or creeper pattern. The item is not used up. It can't be found in survival yet.

## The painting

Hang a painting on a wall with room for one 3 wide and 4 high and you may get IT's own: **The Guardian of Kistan**, by Emelie Stark.

All of these are IT's. Data and Media have none yet.
