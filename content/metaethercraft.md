**METAethercraft**, or **AETHER IS NOW ON METACRAFT**, was an update to the [[Season 4]] survival server on 1 April 2025. It was the season's April Fools' joke.

Despite the name, the Aether dimension was not actually added to the server: the datapack [Islands Overworld Dimension](https://modrinth.com/datapack/islands-overworld-dimension) was. The world map of the dimension is on [map.metacraft.nu](https://map.metacraft.nu), under the name `fkwiod:islands_overworld`.

## Announcement

The following announcement was posted on the Discord server on 1 April 2025:

```markdown
# :glowstone: AETHER IS NOW ON METACRAFT :glowstone:
Remember all those times as a kid when you built a portal out of glowstone and water and it didn't work? Now you can finally fulfil your childhood dreams, because **today the Aether comes to METAcraft.**

We are **100% serious about this.** Over the past two years we have slowly but surely **recreated the entire Aether mod in vanilla Minecraft.** It hasn't been easy, not least because our inbox has exploded with impatient fans (like you!) emailing us day after day about new updates, and of course 128,674 unread Cease & Desist letters from kingbdogz and Mojang Studios AB. But at last the day is here, and you need **no mods** for it. Simply build a portal as shown in the picture below and use a water bucket to activate it!

We know you must have lots of questions about this update, so we have prepared a little FAQ below:

**Q: Is this really the Aether?**
yes

**Q: Will this save my marriage?**
yes

**Q: What will happen to the End dimension you released yesterday?**
what dimension?

And that's all from us! We have already spent 95 billion kronor* on this update and will spend 82 billion more, so we really hope you like it as much as we do. By the way, we are also renaming ourselves **METAethercraft** to celebrate this monumental milestone in Minecraft history.
-# *the Czech kind

See you in the Aether (in METAethercraft)! @everyone
```
