**Brewing** lets you make beer, wine, vodka and the chapters' own drinks. Drinks are brewed in three steps, and most need only one or two of them: **cook** the ingredients in a cauldron, **distil** the brew in a brewing stand, and **age** it in a barrel. The closer you keep to a drink's times, the better it comes out.

## Cooking

{{< image src="booklet/brewing/cauldron.png" caption="A cauldron over heat" width="150" >}}

You need a cauldron filled with water, standing on something hot: fire, lava or a lit campfire.

1. Throw the ingredients into the cauldron, all of them, in the amounts the recipe gives.
2. Right-click it with a stick to start cooking.
3. Wait out the cooking time. It keeps cooking while you are away.
4. Take the brew out with a glass bottle: one bottle per level of water, three from a full cauldron.

What comes out is the drink itself, or an **ingredient mixture** when the recipe still wants distilling or a barrel. Hover the bottle to see which.

The recipe decides the drink, the cooking time its quality. Too short or far too long and the drink is poor, or a failed brew. A poor drink is stronger than it looks.

## Distilling

{{< image src="booklet/brewing/distilling.png" caption="Distilling in a brewing stand" width="200" >}}

Vodka, rum, apple liquor and the chapter spirits come out of the cauldron as a mixture that needs distilling.

1. Fuel a brewing stand with blaze powder.
2. Put the mixtures in the bottle slots and leave the ingredient slot **empty**.
3. Wait for one run. A recipe that says "distil twice" wants a second.

## Barrels

{{< image src="booklet/brewing/barrel_frame.png" caption="An end slice (left) and a middle slice (right)" width="250" >}}

A barrel lies on its side, 4 blocks long and 3 by 3 across, all of one wood: two end slices with two middle slices between them.

* each slice across is round: **stairs** on the four corners, **planks** on the four sides;
* the two end slices are filled in, with a plank in the middle; the two middle slices are hollow;
* it stands on four **fences**, one under each bottom corner of the end slices.

### The spigot

{{< image src="booklet/recipe/barrel_spigot.png" caption="Barrel spigot" width="250" align="none" >}}

A stick and a stone over any planks make a **barrel spigot**. Place it on the middle plank of either end: the frame becomes one barrel, and the spigot is its door.

### Ageing

Put the mixtures in through the spigot and wait. Ageing goes on in real time, a matter of hours for the best drinks, and keeps going while nobody is near. Some drinks want a particular wood; the recipe says which. Leave a drink in too long and it gets worse again.

## Drinking

Every alcoholic drink adds to how drunk you are, more for strong drinks and more again for poor ones. It wears off with time.

* A little drunk, you get weak and slow.
* More, and you stagger and the world spins.
* Much more, and it gets dark and the alcohol starts to hurt. Stop there.

Bread sobers you up a bit, a milk bucket a lot.

The chapter drinks black you out before it gets that far.

### IT's drinks

{{< image src="booklet/brewing/chapter_drinks.png" caption="IT's drinks" width="250" >}}

The chapters' own drinks, from PolymITer, brewed the same way. Each alcoholic one sends you fast or slow (Släggan: slow and strong, or fast and weak), harder the drunker you are, and past a point it blacks you out: you come to somewhere else with a headache, a little hurt and blind for a moment.
