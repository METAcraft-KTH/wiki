A **Portable Jukebox** is a jukebox you carry around.

## Usage

Right-click the Portable Jukebox to open it: one slot for a music disc, and Play and Stop buttons. It keeps playing while you carry it.

Place it and it stays a small block that keeps playing. A redstone signal starts it, and a comparator reads it.

## Obtaining

It can't be crafted. A [[Piglin Drummer]], from the Nether and its [[Rift Raids|rifts]], drops one now and then (5%, plus 10% per level of Looting), sometimes with a disc already in it, usually Pigstep. Some play their music at a different pitch or volume.
