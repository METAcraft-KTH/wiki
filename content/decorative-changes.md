Decorative changes made to the METAcraft SMP compared to vanilla Minecraft. Want to add or remove features? Post on Discord in #general and we can bring it up in #polls!

## Statues

Armor stands can be configured into different poses by using a book named "Statues" (capital S). [See here for a tutorial.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nV9-_RacnoI)

## Mob heads

All mobs have a chance to drop their head when killed by a player. [Exact probabilities.](https://wiki.vanillatweaks.net/More_Mob_Heads)

## Player heads

Players always drop their head when killed by another player.

## Wandering Traders

Wandering Traders sell custom mini-blocks you can use to decorate your builds.

## World generation

World generation uses the datapacks [Geophilic](https://modrinth.com/datapack/geophilic) and [Tectonic](https://modrinth.com/datapack/tectonic) with some private modifications. They give the world a more unique feel without affecting gameplay or progression.

## The End

The End uses [Nullscape](https://modrinth.com/datapack/nullscape) and [Moog's End Structures](https://modrinth.com/mod/mes-moogs-end-structures/gallery) in an attempt to make the dimension a bit less boring. Phantoms now spawn in the End, and you won't need to run to the world border just to find an elytra. [Elytra Vaults](https://modrinth.com/datapack/elytra-vaults-atlasplays) have been added, and there are always shulkers left in every end city.

## Chapter colors

Cerise and Laser Violet have been added to the game, and can be used on all the blocks that other dyes can. They can only be acquired from Sniffers, so get brushing!
