**A New Continent...?** (Swedish: *En ny kontinent...?*), or **the PVP event**, was an update and event that took place in [[Australia]] on 14 October 2024, as part of [[Season 4]].

## Update content

{{< image src="history/cerise_shard.png" caption="A Cerise Shard" width="100" >}}

The update introduced the season's anarchy zone, [[Australia]], where no rules on PVP and griefing apply.

The following new items were also introduced:

* [[Mystifier]]
* [[Cerise Shard]]
* [[Portable Jukebox]]
* The music discs [[spranga-kth|Spränga KTH]] and [[rod-gron-bla|Röd Grön Blå]]
* [[Goat Horn|Goat horns]] with two unique sound effects
* [[Soul Bottle]] (deleted after the event ended)
