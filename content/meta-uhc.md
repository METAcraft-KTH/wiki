**META UHC** was a PVP event that took place on 3 October 2023 in META. It was part of Datasektionen's 40th anniversary, the dJubileum.

The goal was to be the last surviving player or team. The players are dropped into a world with vanilla generation, and have to gather resources to survive and fight the other players.
