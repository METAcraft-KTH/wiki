**Season 4** was the fourth iteration of METAcraft's survival server, running during the 2024/25 academic year. The term sometimes also refers to all Minecraft-related events of that academic year, even those that had nothing to do with the survival server.

## Plot

Season 4 began with Captain O'Brien and his crew hunting for the Flying Swede when they are caught in a storm. Everyone falls overboard in the storm, and the captain's parrot Polly is carried off by the wind towards a mysterious ship on the horizon, while the captain loses consciousness. They wake up on an unknown beach and explore the area. In this new world they find the flying Campus, where Computer Science and Media Technology students live in harmony.

A research project to open the way to the ancient Nether dimension is nearly finished. Dr. Trayorous Junior tries to activate a machine that is supposed to open the way, but something goes wrong and monsters appear. The players defend themselves as the monsters come in waves. Finally the great Wither arrives, and they defeat it by the skin of their teeth. After that the waves seem to stop, and the Nether star that had powered the Wither turns out to be the last ingredient needed to open the way.

After a while a ship falls from the sky and crash-lands in the castle courtyard. The captain rushes to the site and receives a mysterious object from the dying pilot.

## Administration

During the 2024/25 academic year (Season 4), METAcraft was a sub-project of the DESC committee, with its own budget lines. The season had two project leads: one from Data (hexalotl) and one from Media (Ahara). Although the project was not shared between the Data and Media chapters, the Media project lead could join through a guest membership.
