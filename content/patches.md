**Patches** are sewn onto an [[ovve]] or frack, just like the real thing. About 15 patches were added at Season 6 launch.

## Obtaining

You can earn patches at chapter events and in games. A patch you are given goes into your stash with a flourish and a line in chat, and waits there until you take it out.

Everyone who participated in the Metacraft Rivals event has been given its patches.

Your stash is synced across METAcraft's event and survival servers, so patches earned on an event server show up on the SMP too.

## Stash

Open the stash with `/ovvar patches` and hover a patch to see who drew it and where it may be sewn.

## Sewing

Place your ovve or frack on an armor stand to sew patches onto it.

## Advancements

There are advancements for collecting patches.
